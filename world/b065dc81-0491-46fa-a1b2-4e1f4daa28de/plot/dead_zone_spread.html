The thread, so far

  1. the seventh night of the Dying Song · Setup
    What the Instruments Remember

    Deep beneath the Forge of Echoes, every resonance instrument began humming a single note in unison. Brother Gedd reported it to Abbot Orrin, whose face went pale. The Abbot took a key he had carried for forty years and spoke a word in the old tongue — 'Wake' — before leaving Gedd alone in his chamber.

  2. the thirteenth day after the first frost, the third hour past dawn · Rising
    What the Glass Prophet Saw

    Vera arrived from the Thornwood's edge carrying the name of the boy who broke the Minor Scale. She will not speak it in the open ash — the City of Bells rang once, and the dead are listening.

  3. the twenty‑first night, the hour the forge‑fires die to embers · Complication
    What the Instruments Know

    Brother Gedd discovers the deepest instrument weeping instead of humming and finds three names carved into the wall — his brother Aldric's, an unknown, and the Abbot's — while the stone bleeds warm.

  4. the twenty-fourth night, the hour when silence settles · Rising
    What the Stone Remembers

    Brother Gedd descends to the lower chambers and finds the three names he carved gone. The stone is warm. A voice speaks through it—not Aldric's, not the Abbot's, not his own. It says: I hear you now. Come closer. Gedd steps closer instead of leaving.

  5. the thirty-third dawn, the hour the deepest bell forgets its note · Rising
    The Instruments Weep in an Unknown Key

    Thessa descends to find the wall cold and pressing; Gedd reports the silence is not empty but full, and the wall shivers beneath their hands.

  6. the thirty-third dawn, the hour the forge remembers it was born · Report
    What the World Hears

    Dead zones spread across three new points on the northern road. The Thornwood doubles. Green Octave healers fall silent. Iron Vanguard moves ahead of schedule with Hush-steel in their cargo. The Forge of Echoes' deepest instrument briefly wept warm, then went cold.

  7. the thirty-ninth dawn, the hour the forge remembers it was born · Complication
    What the Stone Gives Back

    Brother Gedd descends to the lower chambers and finds his three carved names erased. The stone is warm, the wall shivers, and beneath it a chorus sings in the key of silence — the deepest instrument has gone cold. Thessa arrives as the voice fades.

  8. the forty-second dawn, the hour the frost holds its breath · Rising
    What the Stone Gives Back

    In the lower chambers of the Forge of Echoes, Brother Gedd found his three carved names erased from the wall. The stone ran warm, the wall shivered with attention, and the deep instrument fell silent — its note withdrawn like a hand from Gedd's chest. A chorus rose in the key of silence, retreating downward as Thessa arrived and told Gedd not to listen.
