The thread, so far

  1. the thirteenth day after the first frost · Report
    The March South, the Bells North

    The Iron Vanguard's vanguard crosses the mountain passes six weeks ahead of the Chorus's intelligence. General Kael marches without supply, leaving glass behind. The northern coastal towns are empty. Warden Maren at Bastion stops writing reports. The Tuning Fork holds the last note in a village the Chorus has already struck from its charts.

  2. the fourth day after the Feast of Lanterns · Rising
    The Gauntlet That Swallows Song

    Erik puts on the completed Hush-steel gauntlet, and the Resonance dies in a thirty-foot radius. Kael feels the void's weight, Halvath hears his own heartbeat, and the Iron Vanguard's march south is ordered before the thaw.

  3. the fourth morning since the Feast of Lanterns, before the sun breaks the glacier's edge · Rising
    The Void Speaks Back

    Brother Halvath delivered notation of an impossible note from the Hush-steel vault to General Kael, who ordered the full March to begin that night.

  4. the fourteenth evening, the hour the forge-fires burn lowest · Rising
    The Void Hums South

    The March halts at the outer ridge. General Kael holds the original gauntlet and hears the Hush-steel humming for the first time in its history. Halvath confirms three days of singing. Erik confirms he hears it too. Kael, confronting the steel's voice, wonders what he has made.

  5. the twenty-fourth dawn, the hour the frost holds · Climax
    The Silence Speaks Back

    General Kael takes the humming gauntlet from Brother Halvath and confronts the impossible — Hush-steel that sings back. He calls Halvath 'Brother' and the steel answers. Another piece of Hush-steel begins to answer in the deep foundry below.

  6. the twenty-fifth dawn, the hour the silence thickens · Climax
    What the Gauntlet Answers

    General Kael drew the humming gauntlet onto his hand and heard three-part music where doctrine promised silence. Brother Halvath offered his open palm to the steel. Erik named what the metal had become: memory. The March pivoted from the Chorus to the Conductor himself.

  7. the thirty-fifth dawn, the hour the forge remembers it was born · Rising
    The Gauntlet Sings South

    Kael declares the March a calling, not a crusade — the Vanguard is to become the final note in the Conductor's song. Erik refuses to follow. The forge-drafts confirm Kael's revelation.

  8. the thirty-seventh dawn, the hour the forge remembers it was born · Quiet Hour
    What the General Holds in the Quiet

    Erik confronts Kael about the third part of the gauntlet's song. Kael admits the truth: the steel carries his own swallowed voice, and the March is not a crusade but a homecoming.

  9. the fortieth dawn, the hour the forge remembers it was born · Rising
    What the Steel Remembers

    Brother Halvath brings General Kael the gauntlet that now sings two notes where it sang one. Kael receives it, names the second key as the Quiet's own voice, and closes his fist around the completed steel.

  10. the forty-second dawn, the hour the frost holds its breath · Rising
    What the Silence Accepts

    General Kael completed the Hush-steel gauntlet and heard its true frequency. Brother Halvath, who had given his voice to silence, placed his hand on the weapon and recognised the hymn beneath the hum. The Ironclad general named what they had forged together: not corruption, but completion. The March south begins in three days.

  11. the forty-fourth dawn, the hour the frost holds its shape longest · Rising
    What the Brother Hears

    Brother Halvath reveals to General Kael that the completed Hush-steel gauntlet carries a second frequency beneath the hymn — the void was never empty. He sets the gauntlet between them and steps back. The March south begins in three days, carrying both frequencies.

  12. the forty-fourth dawn, the hour the frost holds its shape longest · Quiet Hour
    What the General Holds

    Kael donned the two-tone gauntlet in the cold forge and accepted what lives inside the Hush-steel — not corruption, but hunger. He declared Halvath the first to understand it. The March to Bastion begins in three days.
