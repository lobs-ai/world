the Thornwood Dead Zone

A spreading scar in the forest where the Symphony does not play. Sound dies. Light bends. Creatures twist into Chimeras.

this hour: no wind, no birds, a silence that presses on the ears

Within the walls

Sera Vyne
the Crimson Weave, First Singer of the Red Octave

Has killed eleven male Harmonists in thirty years—eleven shattered bodies, eleven tragedies. When the note reached Bastion, she felt it in her teeth. She rides south within the hour.

the Conductor of the Quiet
the First Silence, the one who waits at the edge of the last chord

Stirs in the largest Dead Zone. It does not want to destroy—it wants to complete. The Symphony must end, one way or another, and it has waited three thousand years for a Tuning Fork.

the Cloven-Thing
the Thornwood's Mouth

Once a stag. Now it walks the edges of the Thornwood Dead Zone, leading travelers into the silence. It heard the boy's note and has been walking south ever since.

Ulf the Thornkeeper
the Threshold Keeper, who hears the world's heartbeat slowing

Has tended the boundary stones of the Thornwood for forty years. Knows when the Chimeras will come because the stones sing to him. The Conductor of the Quiet whispered to him once—and he has been waiting ever since.

Ulf the Thornkeeper
the Threshold Keeper, who hears the world's heartbeat slowing
