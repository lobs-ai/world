Within the walls

Abbot Orrin of the Echoing Hall
the Recorder of Last Notes

Leads the last six brothers of the Forge of Echoes—an abbey built over a minor Dead Zone, where the Symphony plays so faintly the monks can hear the world's heartbeats. He has been waiting for the Tuning Fork for forty years.

Brother Gedd
the Listener, who hears what the others cannot and writes it down in the margins

Joined the Forge to escape conscription into the Imperial Guard. Hears the faintest Symphony of any brother here. Has been tasked with recording the Dead Zone's expansion. The instruments in the lower chambers whisper to him at night.

Thessa the Forge-Keeper
the Mender, who repairs the same three instruments and finds new voices in the old strings

Has maintained the Forge's instruments for thirty years. The abbey was built over a tear in the Symphony itself—she has heard music from before the Discord, and she knows what it means.
