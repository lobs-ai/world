Within the walls

General Kael the Ironclad
the Hand of Silence, voice of the Vanguard's doctrine

Commands the Vanguard's southern march. Believes absolutely that the Chorus corrupted the Symphony and must be silenced. His soldiers do not fear magic—because they cannot feel it.

Priestess Solve
the Mouth of Silence

Leads the religious arm of the Iron Vanguard—the Order of the Hollow Chord. Preaches that the Chorus caused the Great Discord and must answer for three thousand years of suffering.

Silencer Erik
the Null-Maker

General Kael's personal weapons master. Forges and maintains the Vanguard's Hush-steel arms. Has personally nullified over two hundred Resonance attacks in twelve campaigns.

Brother Halvath
the Stripped, who lost his voice and found certainty in the silence

Was once a Harmonist of the Green Octave before his Resonance was stripped by Hush-steel exposure. Now he preaches the doctrine of silence. Still dreams of the Resonance every night.

Ingrid the Iron Maiden
the Iron Maiden, who walks in silence and has never heard the Symphony

Has killed four Harmonists in battle and felt nothing. Her younger brother was taken by the Red Octave when he was fourteen. She joined the Vanguard to see Bastion burn.
