those presently here

Within the walls

Cantor Selene
the Blue Weave, Mistress of Whispers, advisor to four emperors

Sits on the Emperor's council and has for thirty years. She has watched him cough blood and watched his heirs die, and she has said nothing, because the Blue Octave sees further than any other. She felt the note and she knows what it means.

Emperor Valdris the Hollow
The Emperor Who Cannot Die, though he very much can

Has outlived four heirs and two wives. The Great Game is the only thing keeping him alive—if he stops playing, his own court will devour him before the frost takes him.

Captain Orven Brett
the Last Loyalist

Commands the Imperial Guard's inner circle—forty men who have sworn direct oaths to Valdris, not the crown. There were sixty last winter. The court does not discuss the missing twenty.

Confessor Varn
the Emperor's Ear

Has heard Valdris's confessions for twenty years. Knows every sin, every nightmare, every whispered regret the Emperor has confessed. Has never broken the seal.

Quillon the Bitter
the Bastard's Tutor

Tutors Prince Garren, Valdris's acknowledged bastard, in history, rhetoric, and the art of losing gracefully. Garren is twenty-two and has been promised nothing. Quillon has taught him to want nothing and to smile while he waits.

Thorn the Whisper
the Cantor of Courtesans

Cantor Selene's left hand in the capital. Runs a network of informants through high-class bordellos and bathhouses. Has watched Valdris cough blood for three years and said nothing.

Duster
the Chamberlain, who has dressed four emperors and never seen one naked

Imperial chamberlain for twenty years. Dresses Valdris every morning without seeing his body. Knows where the sealed chambers are and what sleeps behind the walls.

Master Aldric
the Imperial Apothecary, who brews the medicine that cures and the poison that cures the patient

Court poisoner by title, apothecary by practice. Has killed seven heirs in thirty years, each death timed to the perfect season. Knows the Blue Octave has been watching him for a decade.

Seraphine the Hollow
the Courtesan, who takes gold from three masters and secrets from all

Beds heirs and generals and occasionally the Emperor himself. Feels the Resonance in their blood—a sign the Great Discord was meant to affect the bloodline. The Blue Octave pays her wages.
