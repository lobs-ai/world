Within the walls

Warden Maren of the Pale Veil
She Who Counts the Shattered

Second to Sera Vyne in the Red Octave. Has kept the count of every male Harmonist killed in three decades—forty-three names, forty-three silences, forty-three tragedies she whispers before sleep.

Old Tomas
of the Upper City, who has seen which windows go dark and which ones stay lit

Has lit the harbor lamps for fifty years. Knows which shadows move and which ones do not. His grandfather lit the lamps the night Maeven of the Silver Throat drowned herself.

Sister Calla of the Green Veil
of the Harbor, who heals what the sea brings but cannot heal what it hides

Junior healer at the harbor clinic for seven years. Has watched ships arrive with the sick and the dying. Three months ago she treated a hold full of crew who screamed without making sound.

The Salt Crone
the Salt Crone, who sells fish and fortunes to those who ask

Sells fish from a stall near the docks. Also sells prophecy to those who know to ask. Claims to hear the City of Bells ringing on stormless nights. The Chorus has questioned her four times.

Veela the Gilded
of the Docks, who paints the condemned before the Red Weave finds them

Paints portraits of arriving merchants for a copper. Painted the faces of three male Harmonists before the Red Octave took them. Keeps those portraits hidden in a chest she cannot bring herself to burn.
