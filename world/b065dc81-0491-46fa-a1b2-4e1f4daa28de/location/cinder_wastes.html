Within the walls

Asha of the Ember Road
the Burning Silence, elder-scout of the Ash-Walkers

Has survived the Cinder Wastes since childhood. She does not believe in the Chorus or the Empire—they are sounds from a dying world. When she felt the boy's note, she was in the ash dunes, and she wept.

Kael of the Ember Road
the Oath-Bound

Sworn to Asha since she pulled him from a sinkhole at age nine. He would walk into the Quiet itself if she asked. She has not asked. He waits.

Sura the Ember Keeper
She Who Remembers the First Fire

Elder of the Ember Road tribe. Knows the old songs—the ones about the world before the Discord, when the Symphony still played whole. She felt the boy's note and went silent for three days.

Brenn of the Shifting Lines
the Ash Cartographer, who maps the unmappable and buries his failures in the dunes

Family has mapped the Cinder Wastes for seven generations. He is the first to admit the maps are lies—useful lies. The wastes do not stay still. Nothing in them does.

Dust-Eater
the Unnamed, who plays with dead men's bones and dreams of ringing

Too young to have earned a true name. Found a piece of Pale Singer glass and keeps it as a treasure. The dissonance in it calls to things in the deep ash—and sometimes they answer.

Vera the Glass Prophet
of the Ember Road, who sees the dead and hears their last notes

Has seen the end of the world three times and survived each vision. The Pale Singers visited her in a dream before they shattered, leaving her with a warning she has never shared with anyone.
