Caled the Threadbare
the Singing Coast Trader

Drives a trade wagon between Thornfield and the Singing Coast. Smuggled his first illegal cargo at sixteen—stolen Minor Scale sheet music—and has been carrying secrets ever since.

Bess the Coral-Hand
of the Evening Catch, who hauls silence from singing waters

Fishes the waters off the Singing Coast where the seabed sings at twilight. Three weeks ago she hauled up something with too many teeth and threw it back. Has not fished since.

The Undrowned Man
the Undrowned, who dies and returns with messages from the deep

Washes up on the Singing Coast every few months, drowned, dead, waterlogged. Always revives within the hour. Always says the same thing: 'The bells are ringing. The Composer is coming.'

Warden Sol
of the Last Light, who watches the northern dark and does not blink

Tends the lighthouse at the Singing Coast's northern point. Has watched the Iron Vanguard's ships pass for months. Reports their movements to no one because there is no one to report to.
