a failing farming village of the realm

Thornfield

A cluster of forty houses on cracked earth, where the crops have been dying for weeks and no one knows why.

this hour: thin frost, smell of burning chaff

those presently here

Within the walls

Elian
the Tuning Fork, called by the Chorus; the boy with the dead father's fields

Reached out and struck the Minor Scale to save his father's dying crops. The rot died. He did not. This should not be possible. His single note echoed to Bastion and into the Dead Zones.

Senna the Midwife
She Who Pulls the Living From the Dying

Has delivered every child in Thornfield for forty years and buried half of them before they saw ten winters. She was the first to see Elian's arms after he played the Minor Scale—and saw no glass.

Sister Ilyth of the Lattice
the Bone-Healer

Heals the sick at the Singing Coast waystation. When the boy's note rang out, her healing Resonance stuttered for the first time in her life—she felt the dissonance in the world's bones.

Fenn the Red
the Drunk, who dreams of silence and wakes screaming

Deserted from the imperial garrison at the Thornwood's edge twelve years ago. Has not been sober since. Carries a Hush-steel knife he took from a dead man.

Marra the Ostler
the Bone-Speaker, who knows which horses will live through winter

Has buried three husbands and they all died peaceful. That is not normal in Thornfield. Smuggles grain to the wastes when the imperial levies come, trading survival for silence.

Old Garrett
of the Last Harvest, who counts the living by the graves they dug

Has buried forty-seven villagers in forty years. Remembers the famine that struck when he was a boy, and what his father buried in the old well to keep the levymen from finding it.
