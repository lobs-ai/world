Long ago

  1. three thousand winters past
    an ancient
    the Arrogant Caste — the Chord-Breakers

    wove a spell to silence death itself and shattered the world doing it.

  2. three thousand winters past
    an ancient
    Maeven of the Silver Throat — the Last Composer

    wrote the final movement of the Symphony in her own blood and then fed it to the sea.

  3. submerged three thousand years ago
    a lost place
    the City of Bells

    the capital of the pre-Discord world; its towers still ring on stormless nights, and the dead walk its drowned streets.

  4. eight centuries past
    a fallen order
    the Pale Singers

    a brotherhood of minor-key Harmonists who believed they could purify the corrupted scale; they are glass now, scattered across the Ash Barrens.

  5. the moment of the Great Discord
    a broken oath
    the First Silence

    the chord that was meant to end death instead severed the world's resonance in two, and the Minor Scale has never healed.
